Quick guidance

  • Assimilated: commonly 9h daily driving (10h up to twice in a fixed week), 56h in a fixed week and 90h over two consecutive weeks.
  • Break: normally at least 45 minutes after no more than 4h 30m driving; split-break rules may apply.
