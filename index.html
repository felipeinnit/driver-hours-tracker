Quick guidance
  • Assimilated: commonly 9h daily driving (10h up to twice in a fixed week), 56h in a fixed week and 90h over two consecutive weeks.
  • Break: normally at least 45 minutes after no more than 4h 30m driving; it can be split into 15 minutes followed by 30 minutes.
  • Working time: driving and other work count; breaks and periods of availability do not. Do not work more than 6 consecutive hours without a break. For 6 to 9 hours worked, take at least 30 minutes of breaks; over 9 hours, take at least 45 minutes. Each break must be at least 15 minutes.
Assimilated rules / Goods vehicle over 3.5 tonnes Assimilated rules commonly apply to goods vehicles over 3.5 tonnes in the UK and on relevant EU journeys. Check vehicle, journey and exemptions.
